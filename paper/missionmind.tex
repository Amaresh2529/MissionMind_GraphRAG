\begin{abstract}
Investigation boards for spaceflight mishaps repeatedly identify causal patterns seen in earlier missions,
yet finding those precedents in long, heterogeneous reports remains manual. We study precedent retrieval and
grounded root-cause drafting over \PENDING{23} official investigation reports covering 22 missions
(1967--2024). We (i) reproduce a recent multi-field hybrid retrieval method for accident investigation
reports~\cite{kim2026} on this domain, documenting all adaptations; (ii) add an evidence-anchored causal-factor
graph channel whose every edge is tied to a verbatim, page-located quote; and (iii) release a human-labelled
benchmark for cross-mission causal analogy, passage-level causal-factor extraction and question answering.
On the primary comparison, mission-level nDCG@5, the graph-augmented method \PENDING{result vs.\ B3 with
95\% CI and p-value}. \PENDING{one-sentence secondary findings}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Corpus and Benchmark}
\subsection{Corpus}
Official reports (NASA, ESA, JAXA, ISRO) with SHA-256 manifest; page-aware passages whose text equals the page
slice (6,988 passages; provenance verified on all 26 documents). Fields recovered with curated, human-reviewed
page-range maps (Summary/Causes/Disposition analogue), accuracy \PENDING{held-out field accuracy}.
\subsection{Labels}
D2a board-stated factors, D2q query descriptions, D2b graded analogy for 231 pairs, D1 passage factors (200),
D3 questions (\PENDING{n}). Agreement: \PENDING{$\kappa_w$ on D2b; D1 Jaccard}.

\section{Methods}
\subsection{Baselines}
B0 LLM-only; B1 BM25; B2 dense (bge-m3); H-whole; single-field variants; B3 multi-field hybrid with RRF
($k=60$)~\cite{kim2026}; B3-bal (field-balanced, added after observing field imbalance, before labelling).
\subsection{Evidence-anchored causal-factor graph (P)}
Taxonomy of 22 categories; LLM proposals verified deterministically (code, role, verbatim quote located in the
passage); idf-weighted Jaccard mission analogy; graph list fused with B3 by RRF; citation-constrained
generation with abstention.

\section{Experimental Setup}
Leave-one-mission-out; pre-registered primary test (paired permutation, bootstrap CI); Holm correction for
secondary comparisons; local llama3-8B generator; all outputs cached; independent audit.
